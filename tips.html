SMARTER SHOPPING

Six habits worth keeping

Try search →
🔎Compare the final priceCheck the total after shipping, fees, taxes, and any minimum-spend requirement.
🏷️Read coupon conditionsLook for exclusions, expiration dates, eligible products, and whether a code can be stacked.
📈Check price historyA large percentage-off badge matters less if the regular price was recently raised.
🎓Look for eligibility discountsStudent, military, educator, employee, and membership discounts can beat public promo codes.
🛒Compare more than one storeThe cheapest sticker price is not always the cheapest delivered price.
⏰Avoid rushingUnless an offer has a verified deadline, take a minute to compare before checking out.
